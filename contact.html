Frequently Asked Questions

Do you ship across India?

Yes, we ship pan-India via reliable transporters. Packing is done with care to avoid damage.

Can you carve from a photo?

Absolutely. Send us a photo of your old mandir or a reference image, and we'll recreate it in marble.

What marble types do you use?

Makrana white, pure white, Rajasthani sandstone, and green marble — each with its own character.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes! We welcome visitors to our Chandpole Bazar workshop. Please call ahead to confirm.

We'd love to hear from you

Whether you have a clear design or just a rough idea, we're happy to guide you through the process.
