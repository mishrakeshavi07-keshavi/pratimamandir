Our Story

Three generations of marble craft

Pratima Mandir was founded in Chandpole Bazar, Jaipur — a lane that has echoed with the sound of chisels for centuries. We are carvers, not assemblers. Every piece we make is shaped by hand, from raw block to finished moorti.

From Design to Reality

We carve what we draw

1. Concept & CAD

We sketch your mandir or moorti first, then prepare a detailed elevation with measurements. You approve the design before we touch the stone.

2. Handcarving

Using traditional tools, our karigars bring the drawing to life — every arch, jaali and detail is carved by hand, one chisel stroke at a time.

3. Finishing &Installation

The final piece is polished, assembled and packed for safe delivery to your home or temple.
